Low-E Softcoat glass

Designed For Year-Round Comfort

The internal pane uses Low-E Softcoat energy-saving glass to improve the performance of the rooflight throughout the year, helping manage summer heat, retain warmth during winter and reduce damaging ultraviolet exposure inside the room.

Summer comfort

Helps Keep Rooms Cooler

The energy-saving coating helps reduce unwanted heat transfer compared with basic uncoated rooflight glazing, supporting a more comfortable room during warmer weather.

Winter efficiency

Helps Retain Warmth

Low-E Softcoat glass reflects more internal heat back towards the room, helping reduce heat loss through the rooflight during colder periods.

Interior protection

Helps Reduce UV Exposure

The glass helps limit ultraviolet transmission, reducing the exposure that can contribute to fading across flooring, furnishings and other interior finishes.

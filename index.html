British Manufacturing

British Glass. Designed For Year-Round Comfort

All our rooflights are manufactured to the highest standard ready for immediate fitting. Our toughened glass, ceramic border, insulation and sealing specification are brought together during production to create a durable frameless rooflight designed for long-term performance.

Low E Energy Saving Glass

Our Low E glass is designed to keep heat in during winter and reduce overheating in summer, helping improve thermal efficiency and lower energy costs.

20 Year Warranty

All units are supplied with a 20 year warranty and are designed for an expected 30+ year service life when correctly specified and installed.

Two-Stage Sealing

Our insulated units use an internal and external two-stage sealing process, producing a flush finished unit engineered for reliable long-term weather performance when correctly installed.

Factory-Tinted Glass

Our tinted units contain no applied film—the colour is manufactured into the glass for a durable, long-lasting finish that won’t peel, bubble or fade like window film.

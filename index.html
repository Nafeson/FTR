Built for changing seasons

Designed For Year-Round Comfort

The glazing specification is designed to help manage unwanted heat during warmer weather while retaining more warmth inside during colder periods, without losing the daylight and open-sky appearance that make frameless rooflights so effective.

The coated glazing also helps reduce ultraviolet exposure that can contribute to fading across flooring, furniture, fabrics and other interior finishes.

Engineered to last

Factory Sealed And Built For Long-Term Performance

Every unit is manufactured as a factory insulated glass rooflight, ready for immediate fitting. The specification is designed for durability and reliable long-term weather performance.

Ceramic Fused Border

The black border is ceramic infused and fused into the glass during manufacture, providing a permanent and durable frameless finish.

20 Year Warranty

All units are supplied with a 20 year warranty and are designed for an expected 30+ year service life when correctly specified and installed.

Two-Stage Sealing

Factory insulated units use an internal and external two-stage sealing process for a flush finish and engineered leak-resistant performance when installed correctly.

Glass Finishes

Clear, Satin Privacy, Solar Grey and Solar Blue options are available while retaining the clean frameless appearance.

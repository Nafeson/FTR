British Manufacturing

British Glass. Manufactured Into Complete Rooflight Units.

Our rooflights are manufactured in Britain using British glass and supplied as brand new, complete insulated glass units ready for fitting. The toughened glass, ceramic border, insulation and sealing specification are brought together during production to create a durable frameless rooflight designed for long-term performance.

British Glass

Our rooflights are produced using British glass, with the individual panes processed to the specification required before being assembled into the finished rooflight unit.

20 Year Warranty

All units are supplied with a 20 year warranty and are designed for an expected 30+ year service life when correctly specified and installed.

Two-Stage Sealing

Our insulated units use an internal and external two-stage sealing process, producing a flush finished unit engineered for reliable long-term weather performance when correctly installed.

Ceramic Fused Border

The black perimeter is ceramic infused and fused into the glass during manufacture, creating the permanent dark border that gives the rooflight its clean frameless finish.

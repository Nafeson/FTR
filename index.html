Engineered to last

Energy-saving glass. Factory sealed. Built for decades.

Every unit is manufactured as a factory insulated glass rooflight, ready for immediate fitting. The specification is designed for comfort, durability and long-term weather performance.

UV Protected Low-E Glass

Low-E energy-saving glass helps keep rooms cooler in summer and retain heat in winter. It also helps protect interiors from UV exposure and reduces the risk of overheating compared with basic rooflight glazing.

Ceramic Fused Border

The black border is ceramic infused and fused into the glass during manufacture. It provides a permanent finish that supports long-term UV stability, durability and the clean frameless appearance.

20 Year Warranty

All units are supplied with a 20 year warranty and are designed for an expected 30+ year service life when correctly specified and installed.

Two-Stage Sealing

Factory insulated units use a streamlined internal and external two-stage sealing process for a flush finish and engineered leak-resistant performance when installed correctly.

Glass Finishes

Clear, Satin Privacy, Solar Grey and Solar Blue options are available. Each finish changes light, privacy, glare and solar appearance while keeping the frameless look.
